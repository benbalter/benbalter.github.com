- As a member of the inaugural class of [Presidential Innovation Fellows](https://www.whitehouse.gov/innovationfellows), shipped four open-source products from conception to production in six months using then-atypical API-first architecture, including [Project MyGov](https://github.com/presidential-innovation-fellows/mygov), a citizen-centric prototype that let people use a single profile across federal agency sites instead of a separate account for each. The single-account model it prototyped later shipped government-wide as login.gov.
- Described by the US Chief Technology Officer as "the baddest of the badass innovators" and by the White House Director of Digital Strategy as <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uhtlOYOhE8w#t=51m12s">"lightning in a bottle"</a>.
